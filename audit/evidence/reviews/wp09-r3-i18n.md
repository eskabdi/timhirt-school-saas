## FINDINGS

**I18N9R3-1 (Medium): after a failed batch, editing a row shows "Corrections sent for approval."**
- **Where:** src/features/gradebook/GradebookPage.tsx:152, together with :178-179
- **Scenario:**
  1. A teacher corrects two scores on a published exam, and both requests fail (approval_already_pending).
  2. The teacher edits the failed rows.
  3. The `onChange` filter at :152 empties `result.failed`.
  4. The `role=status` condition `outcome === "corrections" && !result.failed.length` is now true.
- **Evidence:** Probe output: `after edit status: ["Corrections sent for approval."] alerts: [] button: Request correction`. Nothing was sent (both requests threw). The scores are still unsent, and the teacher may leave the page thinking they went through. 691f581 did not have this bug.
- **Fix:** Show the success text only when the save itself had no failures and sent something. Store that count when saving, and filter only the per-row `failedBy` map on edit, or clear `result` entirely on edit.

- **Scenario:** After an approval, switching to "My requests" still shows "Approved. The change has been applied."
